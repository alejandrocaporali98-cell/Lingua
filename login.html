What you’ll practice

Pronunciation and diction

Speak clearly and confidently: the sounds Spanish speakers find hardest, rhythm and intonation. Practice with the microphone, which checks every word.

Verbs and tenses

From the present simple to the perfect tenses, always with examples about your own topics.

Phrasal verbs

The two-word verbs used all the time in real English and rarely taught at school: get up, find out, give up, come back.

Vocabulary

Useful words and phrases organized by topic, starting with what you like. Your own word list with audio and search.

Conversation and fluency

Talk more and pause less: give your opinion, tell stories and ask questions, with role-plays in class and on the platform.

Listening

Understand real English at normal speed through dictations, songs, videos and series.
